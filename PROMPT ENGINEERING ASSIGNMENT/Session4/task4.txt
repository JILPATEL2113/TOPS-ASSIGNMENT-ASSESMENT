Prompt
You are a travel planning assistant.

Create a one-day trip plan for Ahmedabad.

Consider:
- Breakfast, lunch, snacks, and dinner
- Local travel between places
- Cultural and historical attractions
- Shopping or entertainment
- A reasonable student-friendly budget

Organize the response chronologically from morning to night.

For each activity, provide:
1. Approximate time
2. Activity/place
3. Food or travel suggestion
4. Approximate cost

After creating the plan, give a short summary of the total
estimated budget.

Provide concise explanations rather than hidden chain-of-thought.

Example Response
🌅 Morning
8:00 AM – Breakfast
Have a traditional Gujarati breakfast such as fafda-jalebi, khaman, or thepla.
Estimated cost: ₹100–₹200
9:00 AM – Heritage visit
Visit Sabarmati Ashram and explore the surroundings.
Estimated travel: ₹50–₹100 depending on transport.
☀️ Afternoon
12:30 PM – Lunch
Have a Gujarati thali at a local restaurant.
Estimated cost: ₹250–₹400
2:00 PM – City sightseeing
Visit places such as Adalaj Stepwell or another nearby attraction.
Travel: Keep extra time for the journey because it is outside central Ahmedabad.
🌆 Evening
5:00 PM – Riverfront
Spend some time at Sabarmati Riverfront.
Estimated cost: ₹0–₹100 depending on activities.
6:30 PM – Snacks
Try local snacks such as:
- Khandvi
- Dhokla
- Sev khamani
- Chaas
Estimated cost: ₹100–₹200
🌙 Night
8:00 PM – Dinner
Have a Gujarati or North Indian dinner.
Estimated cost: ₹200–₹400
9:30 PM – Return home
Use metro, bus, auto, or cab depending on your location.
