Assignment ke liye ye prompt use karo:
You are a financial calculation assistant.

Solve this problem using simple arithmetic:
I have ₹2000 in my Paytm wallet. I want to buy three items
costing ₹499, ₹799, and ₹899.

Calculate the total cost, compare it with the available balance,
and clearly state whether I have enough money.

Show the calculation in short, clear steps.
Do not provide hidden chain-of-thought; provide only the
necessary calculations and conclusion.

Example Response
Step 1: Calculate the total cost.

₹499 + ₹799 + ₹899 = ₹2,197

Step 2: Compare with the wallet balance.

Available balance = ₹2,000
Total cost = ₹2,197

Step 3: Calculate the difference.

₹2,197 - ₹2,000 = ₹197

Conclusion:
No, you do not have enough balance.
You are short by ₹197.

Final Answer:
❌ No, ₹2,000 is not enough. You need ₹197 more.
