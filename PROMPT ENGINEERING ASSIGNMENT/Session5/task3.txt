Step 1 — Product Description Prompt
Write a Flipkart-style product description for a new wireless
earbud called "SoundBeat Air Pro".

Use ONLY the specifications provided below:

Product: SoundBeat Air Pro
Battery: Up to 30 hours with charging case
Connectivity: Bluetooth 5.3
Charging: USB Type-C
Microphones: Dual microphone
Water Resistance: IPX4

Do not invent specifications, features, warranty information,
price, reviews, or performance claims.

Clearly separate the confirmed specifications from marketing
language.

Example Response
SoundBeat Air Pro Wireless Earbuds
SoundBeat Air Pro wireless earbuds offer Bluetooth 5.3 connectivity and up to 30 hours of battery life with the charging case. The earbuds feature dual microphones and USB Type-C charging. With an IPX4 water-resistance rating, they are designed for everyday use.
Key Specifications:
- Battery: Up to 30 hours with charging case
- Bluetooth: 5.3
- Charging: USB Type-C
- Microphones: Dual
- Water Resistance: IPX4

Step 2 — Verification Prompt
Review your previous product description.

For every factual claim, determine whether it was explicitly
provided in my original product specifications.

Answer in a table with these columns:

Claim | Supported by Provided Data? | Assumption/Invented Detail

If a statement is not directly supported by the provided data,
identify it as an unsupported claim.

Do not assume that marketing language is a verified specification.

Example Verification
Claim	Supported?	Assumption/Invented Detail
Bluetooth 5.3	✅ Yes	No
Up to 30 hours battery	✅ Yes	No
Dual microphones	✅ Yes	No
USB Type-C	✅ Yes	No
IPX4	✅ Yes	No
Everyday use	⚠️ General description	Not a provided specification


Conclusion
A verification prompt helps identify which statements came from the provided information and which statements may have been assumptions or unsupported claims.
