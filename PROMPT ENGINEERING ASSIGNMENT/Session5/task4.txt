Original Prompt
Write a review of the latest iPhone.

Problem:
- "Latest" can change over time.
- The AI may use outdated information.
- It may invent specifications.
- It may mix information from different iPhone models.
Improved Prompt
Write a factual summary of the latest iPhone model.

Use ONLY information published on Apple's official website:
https://www.apple.com/

For every specification or factual claim, verify that it appears
on Apple's official website.

Do not use information from third-party websites.

If a fact cannot be verified from Apple's official website,
write "Not found on Apple's official website."

Do not invent specifications, prices, features, reviews, or
release information.

Why does this reduce hallucination?
The prompt restricts the AI to a specific authoritative source and requires it to identify information that cannot be verified instead of inventing it.
