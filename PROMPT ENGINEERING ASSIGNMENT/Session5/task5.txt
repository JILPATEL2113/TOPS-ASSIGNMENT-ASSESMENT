For this task, the important idea is fact + source.
Prompt
You are a movie information assistant.

Summarize the movie provided by the user.

For every factual statement, provide its source immediately
after the statement.

Use reliable sources such as:
- Official movie/studio website
- Official distributor website
- BookMyShow
- IMDb, where appropriate

Do not invent facts.

If you cannot find a reliable source for a fact, write:
"Source not found."

Use this format:

Movie:
Genre:
Release Date:
Director:
Cast:
Story Summary:

For each factual field, add:
Source: [URL]

Clearly distinguish between sourced facts and general summary.

Test Prompt
Summarize the movie "3 Idiots".

For every factual statement, provide the source immediately
after the statement.

If you cannot verify a fact, write:
"Source not found."

Example Output Format
Movie: 3 Idiots

Genre: Comedy-drama.
Source: [verified source URL]

Director: Rajkumar Hirani.
Source: [verified source URL]

Release Date: 25 December 2009.
Source: [verified source URL]

Cast: Aamir Khan, R. Madhavan, Sharman Joshi and others.
Source: [verified source URL]

Story Summary:
The film follows three college friends and explores their
experiences with education, friendship, and career pressure.
Source: [verified source URL]

Important observation
A prompt asking for URLs doesn't itself guarantee that the URLs are real. The sources still need to be checked.
A stronger version is:
Do not create or guess URLs.
Only provide a URL if you actually verified that the page
contains the information being cited.
Otherwise write "Source not found."
