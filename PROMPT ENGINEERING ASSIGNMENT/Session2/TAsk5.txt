Prompt
Explain how to book a movie ticket using BookMyShow.

Use clear headings for every major step.
Use exactly these headings:

Step 1: Search Movie
Step 2: Select Location
Step 3: Select Show
Step 4: Select Seats
Step 5: Make Payment
Step 6: Confirm Booking

Under each heading, provide 1-2 simple sentences.
Keep the explanation beginner-friendly.

Example Output
Step 1: Search Movie
Open BookMyShow and search for the movie you want to watch.
Step 2: Select Location
Select your city or location to see available theatres and shows.
Step 3: Select Show
Choose your preferred theatre, date, and show time.
Step 4: Select Seats
Select the available seats you want to book and continue.
Step 5: Make Payment
Choose a suitable payment method and complete the payment.
Step 6: Confirm Booking
After successful payment, check your booking confirmation and ticket details.
