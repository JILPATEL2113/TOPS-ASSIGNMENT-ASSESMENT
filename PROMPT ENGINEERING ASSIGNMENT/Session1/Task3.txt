Part A — Vague Prompt
Prompt
Write a review for a new café.

Example AI Output
I recently visited a new café and had a nice experience. The café had a pleasant atmosphere and the food was tasty. The staff was friendly and the service was good. Overall, it is a nice place to visit with friends and family. I would recommend trying it.

Part B — Detailed Prompt
Prompt
Write a 120-word restaurant review for a newly opened café
called "The Bean House" in Ahmedabad.

Cuisine: Italian and Continental
Ambience: Modern, cozy, and suitable for students
Price range: ₹300–₹500 per person
Food: Pizza, pasta, sandwiches, and cold coffee
Service: Friendly and reasonably fast

Write the review in a natural and friendly tone.
Mention the ambience, food quality, service, and price.
End with a recommendation for college students.

Example AI Output
The Bean House is a cozy new café in Ahmedabad that offers a great mix of Italian and Continental food. The modern interiors and comfortable seating make it a nice place for students to relax, study, or catch up with friends. I tried their pizza and pasta, and both were flavorful and freshly prepared. The cold coffee was also refreshing. The staff was friendly and the service was reasonably quick. With prices around ₹300–₹500 per person, the café offers a comfortable experience without being too expensive. Overall, The Bean House is a good option for college students looking for tasty food, a relaxed ambience, and a casual place to spend time with friends.

Comparison
Feature	Vague Prompt	Detailed Prompt
Cuisine	Not specified	Italian & Continental
Ambience	Generic	Modern & cozy
Price	Not specified	₹300–₹500
Food	Generic	Pizza, pasta, sandwiches, cold coffee
Target audience	Not specified	College students
Tone	Not specified	Friendly
Output	Generic review	More specific review


Conclusion
The detailed prompt produces a better result because it gives the AI the necessary context, constraints, target audience, and writing style. The vague prompt gives the model very little information, so the response is generic.
