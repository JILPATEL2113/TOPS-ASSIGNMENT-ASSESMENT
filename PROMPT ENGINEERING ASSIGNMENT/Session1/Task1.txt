Task 1 — Unclear Prompt vs Clear Prompt
Prompt 1 — Unclear
Give me a list of trending songs in India.

Example Response
1. Aaj Ki Raat
2. Tauba Tauba
3. Apna Bana Le
4. Heeriye
5. Kesariya
6. Chaleya
7. O Maahi

Prompt 2 — Clear
Give me a list of 10 trending Bollywood songs released after
2022 in India. Include the song name, artist/singer name,
movie name if applicable, and release year. Present the
information in a table.

Example Response
Song	Artist	Movie	Release Year
Tauba Tauba	Karan Aujla	Bad Newz	2024
Aaj Ki Raat	Madhubanti Bagchi, Divya Kumar, Sachin-Jigar	Stree 2	2024
Satranga	Arijit Singh	Animal	2023
O Maahi	Arijit Singh	Dunki	2023
Chaleya	Arijit Singh, Shilpa Rao	Jawan	2023


Comparison
Unclear prompt:
- Output can be broad.
- Required details are not specified.
- The meaning of "trending" is not clearly defined.
- Results may vary depending on the model and data.
Clear prompt:
- Specifies Bollywood.
- Specifies songs released after 2022.
- Specifies artist names.
- Specifies the number of songs.
- Specifies the desired output format.
Conclusion
A clear prompt gives the AI more specific instructions, so the response is generally more focused and relevant to the requested task.
