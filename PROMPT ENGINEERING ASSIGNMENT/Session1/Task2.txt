Mistake 1 — Too vague
❌ Don't
Recommend some food.

The AI doesn't know:
- Cuisine
- Budget
- Vegetarian/non-vegetarian
- Meal type
✅ Do
Recommend 5 vegetarian Gujarati dishes for dinner under ₹300.
Prefer mildly spicy food and give a short reason for each
recommendation.

Mistake 2 — Missing important context
❌ Don't
Book a movie for me.

Important information is missing.
✅ Do
I want to watch a Hindi movie this weekend in Ahmedabad.
Suggest suitable movies and mention their genre, language,
and approximate duration. I prefer comedy or action movies.

Mistake 3 — Not specifying the output format
❌ Don't
Tell me about restaurants in Ahmedabad.

✅ Do
Suggest 5 vegetarian restaurants in Ahmedabad.
Present the answer in a table with:
1. Restaurant name
2. Cuisine
3. Approximate price range
4. Suitable meal
5. One-line description

Quick Revision
Bad Prompt
↓
Vague + Missing Context + No Format

Good Prompt
↓
Clear Goal + Context + Constraints + Output Format
